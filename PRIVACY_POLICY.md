## Summary

Lexi Streak is a word-guessing puzzle game. The app itself does not
collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game progress
(streaks, statistics, and today's puzzle state) is stored **only on your
device** using local storage and is never sent to us or to any server —
there is no account, login, or backend for this game.

The app shows advertising provided by Google's AdMob service, which may
collect data as described below.

## Information the app collects directly

Lexi Streak has no user accounts, no chat or social features, and does
not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone,
photos, or any other device permission beyond internet access (needed to
load ads and send basic analytics events, described below).

Locally stored data (never leaves your device):
- Number of games played and won
- Current and best daily streak
- Guess-count distribution
- The current day's in-progress or completed daily puzzle board

This data lives in your device's app-private storage. Uninstalling the app
deletes it. We have no way to access it remotely.

## Information collected by third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and
rewarded video ads, and to fund free play. Google's SDK may collect and
process data such as:
- Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- Device and app information (device type, OS version, IP address,
  general location derived from IP, app usage/interaction with ads)

This data is collected and processed by Google according to Google's own
privacy policy, not by us directly:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from partner sites/apps:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob's data disclosure: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## Data collected by Firebase Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand basic usage
patterns - which screens are viewed and a small number of gameplay
events (winning or losing a puzzle, your current streak at that point,
using the hint feature). This does not include your name, email, or any
other personally-identifying information. Firebase Analytics may use
device and advertising identifiers for this purpose, processed by Google
per:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
